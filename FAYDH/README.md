## FAYDH Platform(Just documents, work is underway to develop them)a graduation project.

FAYDH is a digital charitable platform designed to unify and organize in-kind donations and direct services through a single, transparent system. The platform connects donors, certified charitable organizations, beneficiaries, and delivery drivers to ensure that essential items such as food, clothing, and furniture reach those in need efficiently and reliably.

The system addresses the limitations of existing charity platforms that focus mainly on monetary donations by enabling structured in-kind contributions, centralized coordination, and clear tracking across all stages of the donation process. FAYDH enhances transparency, reduces operational delays, and supports informed decision-making for donors and charities.

The platform aligns with Saudi Vision 2030 by promoting digital transformation, sustainable social development, and improved governance within the charitable sector.
